# Photoreal Person Canvas

## Purpose

Create a reusable person authority before producing portraits, editorial images,
virtual-model scenes, spokesperson content, or recurring photoreal characters.

## Consent and source gate

Record consent and permitted uses before using a real person's likeness. For a
trained identity, prefer 8-12 sharp photos: one person, visible eyes, no heavy
filters or occlusion, front and three-quarter views, varied lighting, expressions,
and distances, ideally at least 1024 pixels on the short side.

The maintained example uses a fictional adult and does not train or imitate a real
person.

## Topology

`consent/identity contract -> canonical portrait -> angle sheet -> scene variants`

Recommended groups:

1. `01 Consent and identity contract`
2. `02 Canonical identity`
3. `03 Reference coverage`
4. `04 Photoreal scenes`

## Reference rules

- Let the identity asset own face structure, age, hair, and stable proportions.
- Let the prompt own scene, action, camera, lighting, and temporary wardrobe only
  when no approved wardrobe authority exists.
- Test the reference in neutral scenes before complex poses or several people.
- Change one variable at a time when evaluating consistency.
- Use a successful approved output as an additional angle reference, not as a
  silent replacement for the canonical identity.

## Realism cues

Describe a plausible camera position, focal treatment, exposure, motivated light,
skin and fabric microtexture, natural asymmetry, environmental reflections, and a
specific moment. Avoid vague `photorealistic 8K masterpiece` boosters.

## Acceptance

Compare face landmarks, apparent age, hairline, eye spacing, jaw, skin treatment,
body proportions, hands, wardrobe continuity, light direction, and lens behavior.
Reject beauty-filter skin, mixed identities, anatomy failures, or unapproved reuse
of a real person's likeness.
