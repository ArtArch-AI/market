# Creative or Style Short-Film Canvas

## Purpose

Create fashion films, music visuals, experimental shorts, visual poems, art films,
and style-led campaign films without reducing direction to a stack of adjectives.

## Direction contract

Name one intention before choosing style. Make camera, lens, light, blocking,
performance, motion, and sound serve that intention.

Use this prompt order:

`primary camera move -> scene -> one visible action -> movement quality -> physical light -> sound intent -> endpoint -> preservation constraints`

## Topology

`directing thesis -> style/subject anchors -> shot board -> three visual beats -> ordered stack`

Recommended groups:

1. `01 Directing thesis`
2. `02 Visual anchors`
3. `03 Shot board`
4. `04 Film beats`
5. `05 Delivery`

## Maintained example

The example is a 15-second 9:16 fashion film called `Glass Rain`. Its intention is
controlled defiance: a silver-coated adult performer remains calm while water and
reflections become increasingly kinetic. The three clips use a slow approach, a
single turn, and a final locked gaze rather than unrelated spectacle.

## Retake rule

Start simple and change one variable per attempt. Diagnose whether failure belongs
to identity, reference role, action density, camera, lighting, motion, duration,
or audio before rewriting. Keep accepted frames as anchors; rejected takes never
become continuity sources.

## Acceptance

Reject generic cinematic decoration, conflicting camera moves, several unrelated
actions in one short clip, light without a physical source, style drift between
beats, or an ending that does not complete the clip's local visual idea.
